🔏 Own your work — timestamp certification
Prove your creation existed today, in your hands. We take a cryptographic fingerprint of your file — the file itself never leaves your device — and anchor that fingerprint into the Bitcoin blockchain through independent public witnesses. You receive a certificate: this exact content existed at this exact time, presented by your account.
Launch offer: free for signed-in makers. Becomes a paid certificate service at launch.
The traced path: you have an idea that helps the UK and the world → say it to The Factory → The Factory builds it → The Factory certifies your ownership → you sell it in our Marketplace (ownership transfers there; we take a small stated cut) → we can even advertise it for you. Your business, born certified.
The key file downloads locked with this passphrase (AES-256). A thief who steals the file gets nothing without the passphrase. Keep the passphrase in your head and on paper — never in the same place as the file.
Honesty in one line: this certificate is dated proof your work existed in your hands — powerful evidence under copyright and trade-secret law, which protect you automatically. It is not a patent: only a patent gives a monopoly on a method even against independent inventors.
