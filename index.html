ℹ️ How to name your domain
One word, lowercase, then an ending — like mybarbershop.com or flowersoflove.co.uk.
· No spaces, no capitals, no www. in front
· Hyphens allowed in the middle: best-cakes.uk
· Endings we support: .com · .co.uk · .uk · .net · .org · .io · .app · .dev
· Shorter is stronger — easy to say aloud, easy to type.
